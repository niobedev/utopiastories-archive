She was getting mad in anxiety. By evening she started pulling her neck chain from the pulley violently to free her from this place. But the chain did not break. She was crying hysterically. She stopped tying simply after several hours and slept on the ground again, exhausted.

By fifth day morning she had no stamina left mentally and physically to get up. She only stared at the path from were Ali disappeared 5 days ago. Her mind was not working properly. She was losing her consciousness frequently.

It was all hazy in front of her eyes. At evening she could see Ali was walking towards her like a dream. She closed her eyes in disbelieve.

Ali touched Neha's cheeks to wake her up. Neha opened her eyes and saw him. She could not believe her eyes. She tried to jump up but fall down again, tangled in her chains. She gathered her last strengths, held his legs and kissed.

"Please sir, don't leave me here anymore. I don't want to die. I thought you are never going to come back." she was crying hysterically.

"Calm down. I was caught up with some work. It took 2 more days than I have estimated" he told.

Neha was still crying. "pleaseeeeee give me any work. I will do it. I won't complain about these chains anymore. But please don't leave me here to die, I don't want to die" she was sobbing heavily. Ali helped her sit and gave her some food and water. Neha slept after sometime.

Neha woke up fresh next morning as her panic attacks subdued. Ali was making a tent on the other side of the well.

"This is your makeshift arrangement in case of rain till I arrange a permanent place for you." Ali told as he saw her get up.

The word 'permanent' panicked her again. But at least it showed that he had no planned to abundant her in near future.

"Do your daily chores. Then we need to work. "Ali told Neha.

After her daily chores Ali released her from her neck chain.

"Any disobedience will be punished severely" he warned.

Neha knew she could not fight with him, especially when she was chained so heavily. She decided to cooperate. Ali took a thick chain and padlocked one end to Neha's collar. Then he pulled her by the chain forward. Tears rolled down from her eyes as she felt like a degraded animal.

It was not easy to walk in shackles, especially when she did not get a chance to walk properly for so long. She had to pull her leg irons up by holding her linking chain by her chained hands so that did not tangle with her legs.

"Please sir, I cannot walk in these tight shackles on my ankles. I will fell down any moment. "She told, planting. She was so tired of carrying so much of chains.

"To survive in this island you need to work much harder. Walk properly" she shouted while yanking the chain.

Neha was sobbing. But she knew there was no escape. She carried on with tiny hobbled steps. They reached at the end of the jungle line where two carts were waiting for them, full of loads. Ali took her to one cart and locked her handcuffs too the wooden handle by a padlock.

"Push it and follow me" Ali told as he also pushed the other cart. Neha looked at her cart. It was full of heavy iron rods and length of thick metal plates. He started pushing it with all her strength.it was very difficult. The road was also not smooth. She stopped midway.

"I can't do it. It is sooooo heavy" she was panting.

Ali took a whip and started beating her mercilessly. Neha was squirming in pain. She got back to her feet and started pushing it by all her strength. She was crying in pain.

They reached near a cave on the mountain very near to Ali's hut. Ali unloaded the heavy hardware from the carts.

"We need to go for one more round." Ali told as he turned his cart. Neha knew she could not resist, so she started walking behind him. She pushed the empty cart till the end of jungle where Ali filled both cart with heavy loads once again.

Neha pushed her limit this time. She did not want to be whipped. She was breathless with fatigue as they reached the cave again. Ali unlocked her from the cart and lead her to the well again. She slept in the tent for the first time.

Next day Neha could see Ali digging a hole near tent.

"Good morning princess, "Ali told. "I am making anchoring points for your tether. "

Neha was not amused seeing another restraint point for her. She did not want to look at it. She got up with rattling of chains, took some water out from the well and washed her face. Meanwhile Ali made a 3 ft. deep hole in the ground. He took a heavy anchor alike iron rod. It had an anchor like crossed over head and a ring at the end of the tail. The tail was 4 ft. long. He inserted the anchor into the hole and covered it with cement. 1 ft. long tail was over the ground with the ring at the end of it. Neha could never dig that anchor and cement out from the ground even with help.

Neha was not given any work for next two days. She knew Ali was working on something as she could hear metallic banging sound from far away.

"Your new home is ready" Ali told her next morning.

"but before that I need to do something" he told as he dragged a 20 ft. chain from the hut. Neha was scared when she saw the chain and the welding machine again. But she knew pleading would fall on deaf ears, so she kept quiet. Ali unlocked the well chain from her collar and welded that 20 ft. chain as permanent tether on her collar. He held the end of the chain and yanked Neha to pull her forward. He led her to the cave where they dropped the materials from the carts some days back. Just inside the big cave there was a smaller cave like structure with just one narrow entry. There was a jail like barred door covering the entrance.

"Come inside" Ali yanked the neck chain as he opened the heavy barred door.

Neha froze with fear. The place was really claustrophobic. It was almost dark inside even it was bright sunlight outside. Deflected sunlight from the main cave door was the only light which illuminated the place.

"I cannot stay here, pleaseeeeee, it is sooooo claustrophobic. Please let me stay near the well. I will die here isolated" she was crying.

Ali forcefully pulled her inside by her neck chain. The inside of the cave was better than she expected. It was an oval shaped cave, around 10 feet in diameter. Ali already arranged a mattress at the side of the cell. He pulled her to a ring on the wall at the end of the cave and attached the neck chain to this ring. Neha tried to fight with Ali while he was locking the padlock but Ali slapped her hard on her face.

"Please sir, don't lock me here. I beg you. "She kept on pleading but Ali locked the gate with another padlock and left her alone.

Neha sobbed for almost an hour. Then she got up and explored her new habitat. The walls were rough and air was heavier. The neck chain allowed her to walk till the barred gate and hold the thick bars. The chain rattled on the hard stone floor as she moved. The metallic jingle echoed through the cave acknowledging her helplessness. She tried to push the gate; it did not move an inch.

"He was making this isolated prison for me for last few days" she thought. She went back inside and sat on the bed. She could see small stream of water running through between a small gaps of two stones. She held some water on her palm. It was clear and cold.

"You can drink it. It is a natural sweet water source, fit to drink. "Ali told from outside the door. He came back with a metal bucket in her hand. He unlocked the door and came inside.

"This is your toilet. Make sure to clean it every day. "He pointed at the bucket.

"So now I am a jailed prisoner? You were making this barred gate to isolate me in this cave? Or shall I call it a cell?"

"You can call it anything. But it took ample of time and energy to build this heavy door. Try to appreciate it. "

"Why do you need to keep me behind this heavy barred door now? I am already chained. You can just lock my neck chain anywhere. I feel claustrophobic inside this place. "Neha pleaded.

"You will be fine here. Also Indian government gives far smaller cell for terrorists. "

Neha sat down on her mattress. She knew if he had decided to keep her here then there is no way she could convince him otherwise. Ali went out and closed the heavy gate by padlock.

"Don't you have a bigger padlock? I might break this" she told in a sarcastic tone.

Two days passed since she was taken to this cell. She was getting food and water. There was nothing to do except playing with the chains. Neha always felt tired of carrying so much of heavy metal on her body. She used to sleep for the whole day. She was becoming very numb. She missed the open place she was chained to.

Ali took her out of the cave next day. He took her to an open field.

"This is my vegetable garden. You need to plant new seeds and water the old ones. "He told. He padlocked her neck chain to an anchoring point he made in the middle of the field. Neha carried a heavy water bucket and started watering the plants. It was a tiresome job but at least she was happy to be able to stand under open sky again. It was not very easy to pull the entire network of chains every time she moved her hands. But at least it was better than sitting alone inside her prison cell. She was tired by afternoon. She sat on the field and took some rest. By evening she was unlocked and taken back to her cell.

"Sir, please stay for some time. I don't like being isolated and alone all the time "she pleaded.

Ali first locked her neck chain in the wall ring and closed the barred door. Then he sat outside her cell. He gave her some fruits through the bars.

"Please tell me clearly what do you want to do with me? How long you want to keep me here?" Neha asked.

"I don't know dear."Ali told. "But if you are planning to start pleading again I will just leave".

"OK sir. Please don't go. I really don't know what else to talk about. I just don't want to be isolated inside this place. Please understand my situation. "

"See, I don't have any personal rage against you, but the situation does not let me free you right now. Frankly speaking till now you have been very cooperative and I am impressed. "

"What else could I do?" Neha pulled her cuffed hands "I am chained like an animal. And you have whipped me several times for not obeying you. Please sir, I am an educated girl. I have my dreams; I want to be successful in my life. I don't want to be a chained slave for rest of my life. "

"Let go these emotions dear. We all got past baggages in our lives. Try to enjoy what god is giving you. I care for you. "

"Oh please, cut the crap. You even don't know my name. "

"It's Neha. Isn't it?" Ali smiled. "Look, the deal is very clear. You do what I say and you will be spared from punishments. And believe me I know various slow and painful punishments. If you want the hard way, you will get it. And never ever try to insult my pride. I am worse when I am angry" Ali told.

Days moved slowly for Neha. She got use to her daily life. She worked at vegetables garden every day. She started understanding the limitations of her shackles. She was getting used to those chains.

Personal hygiene was really a big issue for her. Her old bra and panties were torn and she had to discard them. The cloths she made for herself were just not adequate to properly cover her modesty. She was very embarrassed to wear them but she had no other options. Moreover when her monthly periods started she had no idea how to tackle it. She was hesitant to tell this to Ali. Ali gave her a rope to tie on her waist like a belt to keep cloths between her legs. He promised to give her Velcro type sanitary pads next time. He also gave her 5 day's leave from field work.

Neha always thanked god that at least she was not captured by a rapist. Ali never looked at her in sexual way even when she wore minimal clothing. But he was a hard taskmaster and Neha got her first punishment soon after she completed one month in the cell.

"You have not watered the plants properly yesterday. Why?" Ali was angry.

"I am sorry sir; I was very tired yesterday after digging holes in the ground for new plants." Neha told in a low voice.

Ali came back with a heavy iron ball .there was a 2 feet chain welded with the ball. He wrapped the chain on her left ankle above the cuff and padlocked it there.

"Perhaps dragging the weight around for a week will make you remember your work next time." he told.

Neha sobbed in silence. She stopped arguing with Ali long back. She had to drag the dead weight for a week. It was frustrating and painful. It was never taken off during the week. She had to carry it even during her field work. Her ankle was chafed. She thanked Ali when he unlocked it after a week. She promised herself not to repeat the same mistake again.

Neha's chances of escaping were fading as the months passed. She became very numb. She was completely dependent on Ali. Ali used to go out once in every month from the island to get supplies. She got some luxury items for her cell like soaps, toothbrush, toothpastes, comb towel, a mirror and Velcro pads. Ali cared for her. He stayed up an entire night and gave her medicines when Neha got fever one day. Neha stopped counting days. Obeying Ali's instructions became her only entertainment. She started cooking for him. At least that gave her some freedom from her boring prisoner life. Gradually Ali became her world as she completed one year in this island.

It was just another uneventful day. Neha was thirsty as Ali locked her inside her cell at night. She tried to take some water from the floating natural water spring inside her cave. To her surprise she realized the water was burning hot! She called Ali for some drinking water but he was already gone. Neha lay down on her mattress and tried to sleep.

Suddenly a big explosion shook her up from her sleep. The ground beneath her feet started shaking. Some stones fall from the ceiling. Neha was scared to death. She kept on calling Ali. Ali ran inside the cave and unlocked her cell door. He also unlocked here neck chain from the ring.

"It's a volcano" he shouted. The earthquake was still on. Ali pulled her out of the cell and ran towards the cave entrance. As soon as they stepped out of the cave another explosion broke the silence of the night. Neha was thrown on the ground with the impact. She was hurt. She got up and looked back. Ali was also lying on the ground. He was not moving.

"Sir let's get out of here. "She shouted in panic. But Ali did not respond. She sat beside him and tried to raise her head from the ground. She suddenly screamed in horror as she saw plenty of blood beside Ali's body. Her hands were also rinsed with blood. There was no respiration. He was dead.

Neha screamed in panic. She was still trying to wake him up. Her mind went blank. Her emotions were not allowing her to leave the person who took care of her for the last one year.

Another explosion rattled her thoughts. She suddenly realized that this was her opportunity to escape from the island. She got up and tried to get away quickly. But her neck chain went taut and stopped her on her way. The other side of the chain was still wrapped on Ali's hands.

Neha unwrapped the chain from his hand as tears rolled down from her eyes. She was unable to understand why she was feeling emotionally devastated by Ali's death. She was free, but she was sad.

Neha shuffled with tiny steps as her leg irons would allow towards jungle. She had no idea how to get out of this damn island. It was dark. She could not see a thing around her. Her cloths were torn and it was just hanging from her body. Her neck chain tangled with her leg irons and she fall several times. The atmosphere became very hot as more explosions were heard. Finally she reached sea shore. Her ankles and neck hurt very badly from being pulled so many times by chains. She was exhausted.

She soon discovered Ali's mechanical boat, anchored on the sea shore. She knew it was the only way out of here. She gathered her strengths and walked towards the boat. She crawled over the wood plank and reached the main deck. The sky suddenly illuminated with a big blast. Neha could see bright red lava floating down the mountain. She dragged herself towards the control room. She even had no idea how to start the engine. After several attempts she started the engine. She untied the anchor rope from the deck. She could see fire engulfing the entire island gradually. The boat was not moving forward. Neha cried in frustration as she tried all buttons and livers. Her shackles were not allowing her to the reach the overhead buttons. She took a broom and started hitting the overhead buttons as well. The fire was gradually coming closer to the boat. Neha was trying desperately without success. She tried to run to the other end when suddenly her neck chain tangled with a leaver handle and pulled it hard. Neha fall with a sudden jerk as the boat started moving. Neha got up and held the steering to lead the boat away from the island. She could see the whole island burning. She fixed the steering in random direction and fall on the floor, exhausted. Her neck chain was still tangled around the leaver which started the acceleration.

"What an irony" she thought as she lost her consciousness.

She woke as sunlight hit the cabin in the morning. She looked out. She could see endless water all around. The boat was still moving.

Neha looked around the main deck. Her neck chain dragged behind her, making ample sound. She was searching for a metal cutter to cut the shackles around her limbs but did not find anything. She found a map but she had no idea where she was right now. She got only 3 bottles of drinking water below the deck and no food.

She waited and waited for any other boat but none crossed her path. 3 days passed, she was hungry, thirsty and almost naked. Her drinking water stock was over. She had no idea where the boat was talking her. By fifth day the engine stopped due to low fuel. Neha was so weak that she could not move a bit. Her mouth was all dry. She was losing her consciousness frequently. She lay there waiting to die.

Suddenly a horn honked nearby. She had no strength to get up and see. She dragged herself with all her strength near the steering wheel, grabbed the wheel and pushed the horn. She held it until she lost her consciousness again.

"Who are you? Had you been kidnapped? Can you hear me? "The doctor started asking questions as Neha opened her eyes. She had been rescued and taken on board by Indonesia navy guards.

" Pleaseeeeee save.. Indian Indian" she could only tell before losing her consciousness again.
