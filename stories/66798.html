As directed I parked in the layby. It was just getting dark, more twilight than daylight The road was quite a car came past quickly, its headlight lighting up the interior of my car. They wouldn't be able to see much I thought, which was a good job.

I was wearing black court shoes with a 6" heel, black seamed stockings, held up by suspenders coming from the red Basque I was wearing under the leather mini skirt and white blouse.

Checking carefully to see no one was passing I opened the car door stepping out onto the road. Quickly closing the door and taking the small plastic bag with me, I walked around the front of the car, my heels clicking on the tarmac, there as described was the pathway leading down into the dark.

I stepped into the dark pathway just as a car came zooming past, there was enough street light to see, it was reasonably smooth going straight for 200 yards. My heels clicking on the hard surface, my heart beating a little faster I walked down until I saw the tree with a yellow plastic band around it. Hesitating I stepped off the path to the left, moving further into the darkness, having to walk carefully as the ground was softer and there were small twigs and branches scattered around. There was a thin tree again with a yellow band around it.

I opened the bag and retrieved the items there, a pair of handcuffs, pair of leg cuffs, keys and a 1" leather collar.

I attached the keys to the d ring on the front of the collar, then fastened it around my neck with keys at the front.

Picking up the handcuffs I attached one to my left wrist, then I knelt down with my back against the tree, my legs either side with my ankles behind the tree, I then took the leg cuffs and clicked one around my left ankle, then with difficulty by twisting my body around the tree reached the other ankle, I did not hesitate, as I was afraid my nerve would fail me, but clicked the other cuff around my stockinged ankle ratching it reasonably tight. Now I was very nearly stuck, the keys attached to the collar my only escape. Then I placed both hands behind the tree, feeling for the cuff dangling from my left wrist with my right hand, then using my left hand pushing it over my right wrist, the click as it closed, my heart beating fast in my chest - there was no escape the only choice was to wait.

I knelt on the earth, I was sweating a little despite the coolness of the evening, my heart was not pounding as hard. I looked around but could see very little, the only noises I heard were the trees moving and a car passing on the road. I hope my master would come, he said he would, but....
