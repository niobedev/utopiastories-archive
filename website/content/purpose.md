The original website [UtopiaStories](https://utopiastories.com) temporarily went down in 2022. During that time, I lost access to my favorite stories that I used to read before bed. I truly missed the collection and the comfort those stories provided.

Because of this, I decided to keep a copy of the stories here to ensure they remain accessible for personal reading and for anyone else who might have enjoyed them as much as I do.

All credits and original rights belong to the author of UtopiaStories (webmaster@utopiastories.com). I am merely hosting this archive to preserve the content that was so important to me and many others.
